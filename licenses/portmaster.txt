PortMaster (PortMaster-GUI) and MinUI PortMaster pak
Source: https://github.com/PortsMaster/PortMaster-GUI (PortMaster); https://github.com/ben16w/minui-portmaster (MinUI/NextUI pak that bundles the runtime below)
Version: unknown (PortMaster Python libraries in pylibs.zip and the bin/lib runtime come from a minui-portmaster release; the PortMaster app itself is downloaded at install time by Xtras from PortsMaster/PortMaster-GUI releases)
Shipped as: Emus/shared/PortMaster/files/pylibs.zip (pylibs/harbourmaster, pylibs/pugscene.py, pylibs/pugtheme.py, pylibs/utility.py, pylibs/default_theme, pylibs/locales), Emus/shared/PortMaster/files/{bin,lib}.zip, disable_python_function.py, ca-certificates.crt (from skeleton/BASE/Emus/shared/PortMaster/files/). Third-party components inside these zips are listed in portmaster-runtime.txt.
