# Third-party art

The main menu console logos (`menu_logo_*.png`) and tool icons (`menu_icon_*.png`) in this folder are rendered from:

- art-book-next-es-de by Anthony Caccese, `_inc/systems/logos/`: https://github.com/anthonycaccese/art-book-next-es-de
- art-book-next-es by Anthony Caccese, `_inc/images/`: https://github.com/anthonycaccese/art-book-next-es

Most system logos in those themes were sourced and modified from Dan Patrick's professionally redrawn console logos:
https://archive.org/details/console-logos-professionally-redrawn-plus-official-versions
